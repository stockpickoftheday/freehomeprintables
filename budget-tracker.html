How to use it

  1. Make your copy. Tap the button above. Google asks if you want a copy — say yes. The original stays untouched, and your copy is private to you.
  2. Type in your income. Paychecks and any other money coming in. The sheet adds it up at the top.
  3. Give every dollar a job. Plan your rent, bills, food, fun and savings until “left to budget” reads $0. That is the whole zero-based idea: nothing left unplanned.
  4. Log spending as you go. Open the Expenses tab on your phone and add each purchase with its category. The Budget tab and the Dashboard update themselves.
  5. Check the Dashboard. See where the money actually went, and what is left in each category before the month ends.

Tips that make it stick

  • Start with last month’s real numbers. Guess less: look at your bank statement and plan from what you actually spent.
  • Give fun money a line of its own. A budget with no fun in it is a budget you will quit. Even $50 planned beats $200 unplanned.
  • Pay yourself first. Put savings in the plan before the fun money, not after whatever is left.
  • One fresh copy per month. At the start of the month, copy the sheet, change the month, and plan again.

Common questions

What is zero-based budgeting?

You give every dollar of your income a job before the month begins — bills, food, fun, savings — until the amount left to budget is $0. Nothing is left unplanned, so nothing quietly disappears.

Is the budget tracker free?

Yes. The Google Sheets template is free — no sign-up and no email address needed. Just open the link and make a copy into your own Google Drive.

How do I get my own copy?

Tap the button on this page. Google will ask if you want to make a copy — say yes, and the tracker is yours. The original stays untouched, and your copy is private to you.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open your copy in the Google Sheets app and log an expense the moment you spend it. The totals, progress bars and pie chart update themselves.

What if my income changes month to month?

Make a fresh copy for the new month, type in that month’s income, and plan it down to zero again. Each month gets its own copy.
