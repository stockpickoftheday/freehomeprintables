About Free Home Printables

This site makes free printables for running a home — meal planners, cleaning checklists, chore charts, habit trackers, and calendars. Everything is free, and there is nothing to sign up for.

Who runs this site?

This site is run by Cory Lewis. It is a small, independent site, built by hand, one printable at a time.

Why free?

The site is paid for by ads. The ads on the pages cover the cost of making the printables, so the downloads cost you nothing. There is no account, no email list, and no paid version. What you see is what you get.

How to use the printables

Click a download button, save the PDF, and print it on letter-size paper at home. Print as many copies as you like for your own household. The sheets are made to be used — checked off, written on, and stuck on the fridge.

Questions or ideas?

Use the contact page to get in touch. If there is a printable you wish existed, say so — new sheets are added when people ask for them.
