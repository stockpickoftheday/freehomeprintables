How to use it

  1. Write one habit in each row. Drink water, walk 30 minutes, read 10 pages, no sugar after dinner — whatever you are working on.
  2. Check off each day you do it. One check per day, in the box for that date.
  3. Do not break the chain. A missed day is fine — two missed days in a row is where habits die. Just start the chain again the next day.
  4. Print a new one each month. At the end of the month you can see at a glance which habits stuck.

Start small

The most common mistake is tracking too much at once. Start with one habit, not eight. When the first one feels automatic — usually after three or four weeks — add the next one. A tracker with one full row of checks beats a tracker with eight half-empty rows.
