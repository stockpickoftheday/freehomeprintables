Fun Christmas Activities for Kids

December is long when the kids are bored. Here are easy Christmas activities that keep them busy for an afternoon — most of them free to print, none of them needing a screen.

1. Go on a Christmas scavenger hunt

Print the free Christmas scavenger hunt and send the kids looking for twenty Christmas things — a candy cane, a gingerbread man, a star on top of a tree. It works indoors on a rainy day, or outdoors around the neighborhood if the weather holds. Give each child a sheet and a pencil, and let them check things off as they find them. The first one to finish gets to pick the next activity, which is a prize that costs nothing and still feels like winning.

2. Count down with one small activity a day

The hardest part of December is the waiting. A countdown calendar fixes that by giving each day its own small job: make a paper chain, hang one ornament, read one Christmas story. Twenty-five little activities spread across the month turn waiting into doing, and the kids stop asking how many days are left because they are too busy with today’s one.

3. Bake and decorate together

Pick one simple recipe — sugar cookies, gingerbread men, or rice crispy treats — and let the kids do the decorating. Icing, sprinkles and a little mess are the whole point. Put on Christmas music, clear the table, and accept that the kitchen will need wiping down after. The cookies do not have to look good. They have to be made together, and they always taste better that way.

4. Take a Christmas lights walk

After dinner, bundle up and walk the neighborhood to look at the lights. Give the kids a job: count the houses with lights, find the biggest tree in a window, spot the inflatable Santa. A twenty-minute walk burns off the cookie energy, and it costs nothing. Bring hot chocolate in a travel mug if it is cold, and the walk becomes the best part of the day.
