How to use it

  1. Plan on Sunday. Sit down with the family and fill in the week. Seven days, four meals a day. Start with the Family favorites box — the meals everyone likes — and drop them into the empty days.
  2. Write the grocery list as you go. Every time a meal needs something you do not have, add it to the list at the bottom of the sheet.
  3. Shop once. Take the sheet to the store and check items off as you buy them.
  4. Stick it on the fridge. When someone asks “what’s for dinner?”, point at the sheet.

Ideas that make the week easier

  • Give each night a theme. Pasta Monday, taco Tuesday, soup Wednesday — themes cut the daily “what should we eat?” argument in half.
  • Plan one leftover night. A night for leftovers means less cooking and less waste.
  • Keep a short list of easy meals. Five or six meals everyone likes, ready to drop into any empty box.
