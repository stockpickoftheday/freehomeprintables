5 Easy Christmas Crafts for Kids

Christmas crafts do not need fancy supplies or a trip to the craft store. All five of these need nothing more than paper, crayons, scissors and glue — and two free printables to get started.

1. Color the Christmas coloring pages

Start with the simplest one. Our free Christmas coloring pages have six pictures — Santa, a reindeer, a Christmas tree, a snowman, presents and a stocking — with bold outlines made for crayons and markers. Print one page per child, or print the whole set and let them pick a favorite. When the coloring is done, the pages make fine decorations: tape them to a window, pin them to the fridge, or fold one into a card for grandma.

2. Write a letter to Santa

The free Christmas activity pack has a letter to Santa to fill in and decorate. Younger kids can draw what they want instead of writing it, and older kids can write a proper letter. Put the finished letter in an envelope and leave it by the tree, or tuck it into a stocking. It is a small thing, but kids remember writing it.

3. Play tic-tac-toe, Christmas style

The same activity pack has a tic-tac-toe board to print and play. Use coins, buttons or little wrapped candies as the pieces, and play best of three while the cookies bake. Print a few copies and the game can go on all afternoon without anyone asking for a screen.

4. Make paper snowflakes

Fold a square of white paper in half, then in half again, and cut small triangles out of the folded edges. Open it up and every snowflake comes out different, which is the whole point. Hang them in a window with tape or string them together into a garland for the mantel. If the first one tears, that is normal — the second one always looks better.

5. Turn a coloring page into a Christmas card

Once a coloring page is finished, fold a sheet of card in half and glue the colored picture to the front. Write a message inside and you have a handmade card that costs nothing and means more than a bought one. Kids can make one for each grandparent, teacher or neighbor, and each card is different because each picture is different.
