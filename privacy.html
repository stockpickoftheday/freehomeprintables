Privacy policy

This site does not ask for your name, your email, or any account. You download a file and print it — that is the whole transaction.

What this site collects

Nothing from you directly. There is no sign-up, no login, and no form that asks for personal information. Downloading a PDF does not require giving us anything.

Cookies and ads

This site shows ads through Google AdSense, and may use analytics to see which pages people visit. Those services may use cookies to show ads and count visits. We do not sell personal information to anyone, and we do not have any personal information to sell.

Email

If you email us through the contact page, your email address is used only to reply to you. It is not added to any list and it is not shared with anyone else.

Children

This site is made for adults running a household. It does not knowingly collect information from children, and it does not ask anyone for personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here. Last updated November 2026.
