Play the Christmas crossword puzzle online — free, no sign-up

Twelve Christmas words to solve — across and down. Tap a square, type a letter, and the puzzle fills in as you go. It plays right in your browser on your computer or phone, with nothing to print and nothing to download.

How to play

  1. Tap a square to pick a word — tap the same square again to switch between across and down.
  2. Type the letters. The puzzle moves to the next square on its own.
  3. Tap Check to see which letters are wrong, Reveal to fill the whole puzzle, or Clear to start over.
