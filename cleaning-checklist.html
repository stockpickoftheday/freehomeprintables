How to use it

  1. Do the daily list every evening. It takes about 15 minutes: beds, dishes, counters, trash, a quick tidy.
  2. Pick one weekly day. Saturday morning is the classic choice. Work down the weekly list in order.
  3. Do the monthly list on the first of the month. Spread it across the first weekend if you like — it does not have to be one day.
  4. Check things off. Checking a box is the reward. Cross a task off and move on.

What is on the sheet

  • Daily (7 tasks): beds, dishes, counters, trash, tidy, bathroom sink, sweep the kitchen.
  • Weekly (8 tasks): vacuum, mop, bathrooms, dusting, sheets, laundry, appliances, trash cans.
  • Monthly (8 tasks): windows, baseboards, fridge, under furniture, fans and vents, oven, shower curtain and grout, smoke detectors.
