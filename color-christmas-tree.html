Common questions

How do I color the tree?

Pick a color from the palette, then tap any part of the picture — the tree, a bauble, the star, a present — and it fills in with that color. Tap a part again with a different color to change it.

Do I need a printer?

No. The picture colors right here on the page, in your browser. Nothing to print and nothing to download.

Does it save my colored tree?

No — your colors stay on this page only while it is open. If you refresh or close the page, the tree goes back to blank so you can color it again.

Is it free?

Yes. Free to color as many times as you like — no sign-up and no email address needed.
