How to print wall art

  1. Download the print you want. Click the download button under it — the file is a ZIP with a high-resolution JPG inside. Open the ZIP to get the JPG.
  2. Print at 100%. Do not use “fit to page” — the files are already sized to print sharp at the size listed under each one.
  3. Use good paper. Matte photo paper or heavy matte paper (at least 200 gsm) makes the colors look richest. Regular printer paper works too.
  4. Use your printer's best quality setting. It takes a little longer, but the print comes out noticeably sharper.
  5. Frame it. Any standard frame in the print's size works — the tall prints fit narrow frames.

Common questions

Are the Christmas wall art prints really free?

Yes. Every print on this page is free to download and print for your own home. No sign-up and no email address needed.

What do I get when I download a print?

A ZIP file with a high-resolution JPG inside, at 300 DPI. The print size is listed under each one. The cat print’s ZIP holds five sizes, up to 14 by 11 inches.

What paper should I use for wall art?

Matte photo paper or heavy matte paper (at least 200 gsm) looks best. Regular printer paper works too, but the colors look richer on photo paper. Print at your printer’s best quality setting.

Can I sell these prints?

No. The prints are free for your own home use only — print as many copies as you like for yourself and your family, but do not sell them or give them away as a product.
