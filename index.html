How printables work

Most printables on this site are PDF files. Click the download button, save the file, and print it on regular letter-size paper (8.5 by 11 inches) at home or at a copy shop. The wall art prints are high-resolution JPG files instead — each one lists its own print size on the wall art page. They are black-and-white friendly, so they look fine on any printer. Print a fresh copy whenever you need one — a new meal planner every week, a new habit tracker every month.

Why is everything free?

This site is paid for by ads. The ads you see on the pages cover the cost of making the printables, so the downloads cost you nothing and always will. There is no account to create, no email list to join, and no premium version hiding behind a paywall.

Printing tips

  • Use letter paper. Every sheet is made for US letter size, 8.5 by 11 inches.
  • Print at 100%. Do not use “fit to page” — the sheets are already sized to fit.
  • Pin it where you will see it. The fridge is the classic spot for a meal planner and a chore chart. A habit tracker works best on a desk or a bedroom wall.
  • Use a pen, not a pencil. A checked-off box feels better in ink, and you cannot erase a good streak.

Everything on this site is for your own home use. Print as many copies as you like for yourself and your family.
