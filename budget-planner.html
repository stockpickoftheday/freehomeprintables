How to use it

  1. Write the month at the top. One sheet per month.
  2. Fill in each expense. Rent, bills, groceries, and everything else — plus two blank rows for anything of your own.
  3. Add your debt payments, then write down your monthly income.
  4. Fill in savings and investments, then check the summary at the bottom for the money left over.

Start with the big three

Rent, groceries, and transport are where most of the money goes. Get those three right and the rest of the sheet mostly takes care of itself. If the same category keeps running over, that is the one to fix first — not the whole budget.
