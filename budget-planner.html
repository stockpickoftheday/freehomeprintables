How to use it

  1. Write the month and your income at the top. Paycheque plus anything else coming in, then the total.
  2. Plan each expense before the month starts. Fill in the Planned column for rent, bills, groceries, and everything else.
  3. Fill in the Actual column as the month goes on. Write what you really spent, and the Difference column shows the gap.
  4. Check the money left over at the bottom. Whatever is left goes straight to savings — even $20 a month adds up.

Start with the big three

Rent, groceries, and transport are where most of the money goes. Get those three planned right and the rest of the sheet mostly takes care of itself. If the Difference column keeps showing the same category over, that is the one to fix first — not the whole budget.
