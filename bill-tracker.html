What it looks like

A bills table with due dates and paid checkboxes, a total-due line, and a debt payoff section underneath.

How to use it

  1. Write the month at the top.
  2. List every bill with its due date and amount, then tick the paid box when it is paid.
  3. Add up the total due this month on the total line.
  4. List your debts in the payoff section with each balance, payment, and payoff date — update the balances every month.

Common questions

How many bills fit on the tracker?

What is the debt payoff section for?

Five rows to list each debt with its balance, your monthly payment, and the date you expect to pay it off — so you can watch the balances fall month by month.
