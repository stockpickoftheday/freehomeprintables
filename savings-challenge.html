How to use it

  1. Pick a start date and write it on the sheet. January 1st is the most popular start, but any Monday works — the sheet is undated.
  2. Put that week's amount aside. Week 1 is $1, week 2 is $2, and so on. An envelope, a jar, or a savings account all work.
  3. Check the box. The running total is printed next to each week, so you always know where you stand.
  4. Keep going to week 52. The amounts feel small at the start and the total grows faster than you expect — $351 by the halfway point, $1,378 at the end.

Why it works

The first weeks are almost nothing — $1, then $2, then $3. That is the whole point. Anyone can start, and by the time the amounts get real, saving every week is already a habit. The printed total next to each week does the maths for you, so there is nothing to work out.
