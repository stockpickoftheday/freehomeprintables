How to use it

  1. Write a budget for each category before you start shopping.
  2. Fill in the Spent column as you buy things.
  3. Work out what is Left in each row so you know what is still to spend.
  4. Add up the total at the bottom to see what Christmas cost.

Tips to stay on budget

  • Set the total first, then split it across the categories — not the other way round.
  • Write down every gift before you shop, so you buy what is on the list and nothing else.
  • Fill in the Spent column as you go — a budget only works if you keep it up to date.
  • Homemade gifts and baking cost less and mean more than something from a shop.
  • Buy next year's decorations in the January sales, when they are half price or less.
