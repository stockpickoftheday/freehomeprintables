Free Printable Christmas Budget Tracker

Keep Christmas spending under control: write down who gets a gift, your gift idea, what you plan to spend, and what you actually spent — then add it all up at the bottom.

Download the free Christmas budget tracker PDF

Free PDF, letter size, 1 page, prints at home. No sign-up, no email address needed.

What it looks like

Ten gift rows — who gets what, gift ideas, what you plan to spend, and what you actually spent — with a Total Amount Spent box at the bottom.

The printed PDF is blank, ready for your own gifts and numbers.

How to use it

  1. Write who each gift is for and your gift idea, before you start shopping.
  2. Write what you plan to spend on each gift.
  3. Fill in what you actually spent as you buy things.
  4. Add up the total in the Total Amount Spent box at the bottom to see what Christmas cost.

Tips to stay on budget

  • Set the total first, then split it across your gifts — not the other way round.
  • Write down every gift before you shop, so you buy what is on the list and nothing else.
  • Fill in what you actually spent as you go — a budget only works if you keep it up to date.
  • Homemade gifts and baking cost less and mean more than something from a shop.
  • Buy next year's decorations in the January sales, when they are half price or less.
