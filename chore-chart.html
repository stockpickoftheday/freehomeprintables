How to use it

  1. Write in each person’s name. The chart has room for six people.
  2. Write the chores in the list at the bottom. Dishes, trash, vacuuming, laundry — whatever your house needs.
  3. Give each person their chores for the week. One or two a day is plenty for kids; adults take the rest.
  4. Check off each day. A checked box is done. An empty box at the end of the week is easy to see.
  5. Rotate on Sunday. Swap the chores around each week so nobody is stuck with the same job forever.

Tips that actually work

  • Let each person pick one chore they like. It gets done faster when it is not hated.
  • Keep the list short. Three or four chores per person is enough. A chart with twenty jobs gets ignored.
  • Praise the checked boxes. A full row of checks is worth a word of praise — or a small treat at the end of the week.
