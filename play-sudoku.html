Play Sudoku online — free, no sign-up

One easy Sudoku puzzle to solve, right in your browser. Tap a square, tap a number, and fill in the grid. It plays on your computer or phone, with nothing to print and nothing to download.

How to play

  1. Tap a square to pick it. The numbers already printed in the grid are fixed — you cannot change them.
  2. Tap a number on the number pad below the grid, or type it on your keyboard. Each row, column, and 3-by-3 box must have the numbers 1 to 9, each exactly once.
  3. Made a mistake? Tap the square and tap a different number to replace it, or tap Erase to clear it.
  4. Tap Check to see which numbers are wrong, or Clear to empty all the numbers you entered and start over.

Easy

You finished it! Every number is right.

Common questions

Do I need a printer to play?

No. The puzzle plays right in your browser on your computer or phone. Tap a square and tap a number — nothing to print and nothing to download.

Is the Sudoku free?

Yes. It is free to play, with no sign-up and no email address needed.

How hard is the puzzle?

Easy. Forty-two numbers are already filled in, and the rest can be worked out with simple logic — no guessing needed.
